Module 4 — Failure Demonstration: Wrong Destination Port

Test command:
curl -i --max-time 5 https://app.cnproject.test:8444/

Observed result:

curl: (7) Failed to connect to app.cnproject.test port 8444
after 5 ms: Couldn't connect to server

Diagnosis:

The client attempted to establish an HTTPS connection to port 8444.

The project HTTPS service listens on port 443, not 8444.
No service was listening on the requested destination port.

Layer affected:
Transport / TCP layer

Correct destination:
app.cnproject.test:443

Recovery:
Use the configured HTTPS destination port 443.
